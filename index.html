Privacy Policy for Image Compressor

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Image Compressor ("the app") is developed by Gyanadipta Pahi. This privacy policy explains how the app handles your data.

1. Information We Access

Photos and Storage: The app accesses photos you select from your device storage in order to compress them. Compressed images are saved back to your device storage. We do not upload, transmit, or store your photos on any external server — all processing happens locally on your device.

Camera: If you choose to take a photo directly within the app, we access your device camera for that purpose only. Captured images are processed the same way as gallery photos — locally, and not uploaded anywhere.

2. Advertising

This app uses (or will use) Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as Advertising ID), general location, and usage data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. This data is collected and processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization).

3. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or share your name, email, contacts, or any personally identifiable information. We do not sell user data to third parties.

4. Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions:

  • Storage/Media access — to read and save images
  • Camera — to capture photos for compression

These permissions are used solely for the app's core functionality and are not used to collect data for any other purpose.

5. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
