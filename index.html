Privacy Policy for Image Compressor

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Image Compressor ("the app") is developed by Gyanadipta Pahi. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use its image compression and related features.

1. Photos and Image Processing

The app allows you to select images using Android's system photo picker. The app accesses the images you select to perform the requested image-processing operations.

Image processing is performed locally on your device. The app's image-processing functionality is not designed to upload your selected images to a developer-operated server.

When you save a compressed image, the resulting file is saved to your device's image storage. Saved files remain on your device until you or another application deletes them. You can manage these files through your device's gallery or file manager.

Access to a selected image depends on the permissions granted by Android. You may need to select an image again if access is no longer available.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google and its advertising services may process certain information when ads are requested, displayed, or interacted with.

Depending on the SDK configuration, device settings, consent status, and applicable requirements, this information may include advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, app interactions, diagnostic information, and other information needed to deliver and measure advertisements.

Google may use information for purposes such as providing and measuring advertisements, preventing fraud and abuse, diagnosing technical issues, and personalizing advertising where permitted. Information may be processed by Google and relevant advertising service providers in accordance with their applicable policies and settings.

For more information about Google's data practices, please read the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Mobile Ads privacy guidance.

You can review available advertising and privacy controls in your device settings and respond to any consent choices presented where applicable. The availability and effect of these controls may vary by device, region, and advertising configuration.

3. Information Collected by the Developer

The app's core image-processing functionality does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or contacts. The developer does not operate a server to receive your selected images as part of the normal image-compression workflow.

However, third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads may process information as described in the Advertising section. Therefore, this policy does not mean that no information is processed or transmitted by any third party.

4. Permissions and Access

The app uses Android's system photo picker to let you choose images. Android controls access to the selected content.

The app may use network access to load advertisements and communicate with the relevant advertising services. Advertising-related device identifiers may also be subject to Android settings and applicable consent requirements.

The app does not require broad access to all photos merely to let you select images through the system photo picker. Actual access depends on the Android version and the features available in your installed version of the app.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Images selected for processing are handled locally by the app. Compressed images saved to your device are retained until you delete them using your gallery or file manager. The developer does not provide a remote image-storage service as part of the normal compression workflow.

Information processed by Google Mobile Ads is subject to Google's applicable retention practices and settings. The developer does not control all retention or deletion operations performed by Google. For details, consult Google's privacy documentation linked above.

6. Sharing Images

If you choose to share a compressed image, Android may open a system sharing interface so you can select another app or recipient. The image is then handled according to your choice and the selected app's own privacy practices. The developer does not control how third-party apps process content you choose to share with them.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is not specifically directed at children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an account-registration system. Advertising and data-handling requirements may differ depending on the app's declared audience and applicable laws.

8. Security

The app processes selected images locally for its core functionality and uses Android's provided mechanisms for accessing and saving content. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the app, its features, advertising services, or applicable requirements. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
